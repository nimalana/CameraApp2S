# Privacy Policy for Microscope Viewer Camera

**Last Updated: July 11, 2026**

## Overview

Microscope Viewer Camera ("the App") is developed by Nimalan Arulvelan. This privacy policy explains how the App handles information when you use it, including if you use the App in France, Italy, or another country in the European Union or European Economic Area.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect personal data.** The App operates on your device and does not transmit photos, videos, audio, settings, identifiers, analytics, or other personal information to external servers.

Because the App does not collect personal data, it does not sell personal data, share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use personal data for profiling.

## Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, all of which are used solely on your device:

- **Camera**: Used to capture photos and videos through a microscope eyepiece adapter. Camera data is not transmitted off your device by the App.
- **Microphone**: Used only if you record audio alongside video, such as narrating what you are observing. Audio is stored in the video files you save and is not transmitted off your device by the App.
- **Photo Library**: Used to save captured photos and videos to your photo library, and to browse or review microscope photos and videos on your device.

## Data Storage

Images, videos, audio recorded with videos, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard iOS frameworks, including the Photos framework. The App does not store this content on external servers.

You control any sharing of captured media through iOS features, such as Photos, AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or other apps you choose to use.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## European Union and EEA Users

For users in France, Italy, and the rest of the European Union or European Economic Area, the App is designed to avoid collecting personal data. Since the App does not collect or process personal data on external servers, requests such as access, correction, deletion, portability, or objection generally apply to data stored on your own device and can be handled by deleting media or the App from your device.

If you contact the developer for support, any information you choose to provide in that communication will be used only to respond to your request.

## Educational and Non-Diagnostic Use

The App is intended for educational, hobbyist, documentation, and general microscope imaging use. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, prevent, or monitor any disease or medical condition, and it is not a medical device.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. In the European Union, where parental consent rules may apply to children under the relevant local age of digital consent, the App is designed not to collect personal data from children or adults.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
