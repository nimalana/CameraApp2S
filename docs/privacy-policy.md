# Privacy Policy for Microscope Camera

**Last Updated: May 1, 2026**

## Overview

Microscope Camera ("the App") is developed by Nimalan Arulvelan. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.** The App operates entirely on your device and does not transmit any information to external servers.

## Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, all of which are used solely on your device:

- **Camera**: Used to capture images and videos through your device's camera. Camera data is never transmitted off your device.
- **Microphone**: Used to record audio when capturing videos. Audio data is never transmitted off your device.
- **Photo Library**: Used to save captured images and videos to your photo library, and to display your existing photos and videos within the App.

## Data Storage

All images, videos, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard Photos framework. No data is stored on external servers.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
